Instructional Zones & Form Highlights

Visual showcase of highlighted fretboard regions, D Form triads, convex hulls, sequence paths, and range braces for guitar instructors.

1. CAGED D Form Triad (Box Zone)

Highlighting the D Major triad block on strings 1..3 with a translucent box zone.

2. Free-Form Convex Hull (Custom Points)

Wrapping discrete fingering points with a smooth rounded polygon hull (type: 'hull').

3. Arpeggio Sequence Line Path

Connecting sequential note markers with a directional line path (type: 'path').

4. Fret Position Range Accolade (Brace)

Displaying a top curly brace / accolade over frets 5 to 8 for scale position bounds (type: 'brace').
